# Privacy

SDR Command Center Lite is made by Csaba Fehér. **The author does not collect, receive or store any data from you or from your use of this plugin.** There is no server, no analytics and no tracking.

## Where your data goes

- **Setup.** When you ask for it, the assistant reads – inside your own account – your past chats (only if chat search is available; it tells you first and you can say "skip chats"), your company website, competitor websites and, if you connected it, HubSpot (read-only). It uses this only to write your playbook and call scripts.
- **Your desk.** Leads, calls, notes, imported files and setup versions are stored in your browser, on your device, separately for each account. They are not sent to the author or to anyone else. Exports and backups go only where you save them.
- **Shared setup.** The company setup and playbook are kept in the page's own database in your account and are visible to the people you share the page with.
- **HubSpot.** Optional. Import is read-only. Calls and follow-up tasks are written to your HubSpot only if you switch write-back on.
- **Other services.** The page downloads its font from Google Fonts. Links to your dialer, mail app, Gmail or Outlook, LinkedIn and booking tool open only when you click them.

Your use of the assistant itself is covered by its provider's own terms and privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

Use **Setup → Reset everything** on the page, or clear the page's data in your browser. Deleting the page removes its database.
